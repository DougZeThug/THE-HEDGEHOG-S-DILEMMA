Tom, the opening and the ending

Tom read the first and last groups of the fable twice each. Choose one take for each group. The lines borrowed from the neighbouring groups are already cut away, so this is the reading the film would use.

How to listen

  • Once through, on the phone's own speaker, not headphones.
  • The opening: someone telling it rather than performing it. The list read as a list, and "The moon began waiting for him." landing without being pushed.
  • The ending: "The moon looked too." should stay open, not settle like the last line of a story. Each ending take runs on into 3 seconds of silence and the room's first laugh, so you hear Tom hand over to the room.

Every take says the text word for word (checked against a transcript), and both takes of a group play at the same loudness. The laugh sits a little under him, as planned for the room. The longer silences between paragraphs come later, in the edit, so judge the voice and the reading rather than the pace. Your choices stay on this device.

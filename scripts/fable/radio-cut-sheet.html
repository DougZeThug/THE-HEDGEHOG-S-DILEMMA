How to listen

  • Once straight through, on the phone's own speaker. Then again with the script, marking any silence that feels too long or too short.
  • The silences are the plan's starting lengths, each shown in the script: the curtains before he speaks, about a second between paragraphs, and longer only where the plan gives time (the waiting, the ease, his request, the rest, her obscured reflection, the touch and the last glance).
  • The night air is very low on purpose. It sits about 21 LU under his voice, which keeps the loudness range inside the plan's 8 LU. If you want more night, say so.
  • At the end the night stops with the image, then 3 seconds of true silence follow, where the title will appear.

# Approach reviews: the visit's heart

Three specialists reviewed `brief.md` and the prototype (`?exp=heart,bookend`) independently, read-only. Their findings, condensed; evidence paths are theirs. The skeptic's challenge is below (*Skeptic*); the decisions are in `../2026-11-emotional-arc.md`.

## Creative director

**Verdict.** Keep the heart: "the first proposal that turns a visit into one sentence instead of a run of separate events." Choosing is safe (each visitor sees one heart and can't know there were others). The words are the risk: they say the hidden rhyme out loud.

1. [P1, verified] "I wanted you to stay." already has an answer: "I didn't know how." (`encounters.ts`, second meeting). In 101 of 114 visits with a heart answer, both were there: a three-line confession; "I didn't know how. / You did, for a while." reads as "you did know how". Write each answer as the line that follows "I didn't know how."
2. [P1] "You did, for a while." is the mirror: the only answer about the visitor; grants the wish; after the woodland it captions the empty clearing next to its own coda "we had all afternoon" (decision 118 refused exactly that). The woodland writes no answer; its coda is its answer.
3. [P1] "I said goodnight instead." restates Things a third time; the film is a leaving with a packed bag. Proposed: "I asked if you had everything." "I didn't ask." is abstract and twins "I didn't know how."; proposed: "I made the bed." Keep "I took my hand back." (the room owns its flinch).
4. [P1] The card should show only what the wall shows. At 3 minutes the heart is seen in 42% but its answer written in 9%; putting it on the card anyway is the room explaining its peak on the way out. First line as the visit left it ("I wanted you to" if unfinished); the answer under it only if the wall has it.
5. [P1] The kitchen light should follow the heart, not give way to it (48% → 17% of 10-minute visits). After Almost, Things or the bedroom, under its usual late gates, 120 s after the heart's aftermath; never after the woodland.
6. [P2] On a return the kept answer is watched at the hand-off (they face the first line); write it only once they've turned from that wall.
- Mappings: keep all four. Almost after the recoil is real recognition; Things after walking away close behind; the woodland looks most like a reward (drop its answer); the bedroom is the weakest rhyme, but the door is unreachable and its image is already a departure line.

## Interaction designer

**Verdict.** The heart solves reach in the battery, but much of that comes from the simulated visitors. Point at the heart only when the visitor's eyes are on a wall, and once more if they miss it.

1. [P1, verified in the browser] At the chair the offered line is written out of view: the placement ray from the screen centre misses the wall when looking down at the seat (desktop: above the frame; phone: out of frame even looking level; kneeling: top-left edge), and it landed 25 cm high among ~10 lines. Twice it came in the same tick as a voice: `heartFree` drops `free`'s 4 s voice check. Gate on looking at a wall (pitch within ±0.35, a wall faced and still ≥1.5 s, no voice in 4 s); place with a level ray at 0.9–1.8 m.
2. [P1] One pointing, then a 150 s block: 44–54% of offered hearts never begin. Re-ink once when they next rest on that wall (≥30 s after); lapse at 90 s.
3. [P1] The recoil and Almost don't line up. The recoil fires after 4 s away; `cameBack` needs 20 s away and a stay of 8 s. In 65 visits the room recoiled before the shape was decided; 28 got another heart (17 Things). A visitor who steps back from the recoil fails "stayed 8 s". Make "came back" = the room has recoiled at them; offer Almost at the first look at a wall ≥10 s after the recoil's hold.
4. [P1] Decide once, but at the pointing, not at the first rule that qualifies ('close' fixed at 60 s in 77% of its cases; shape no longer holds when the heart begins in 17–24%). Letting it change until the pointing: mismatch 10–17%, recoiled visitors getting Almost 37 → 46, reach unchanged.
5. [P1] The answer doesn't last (`ttl: 60`; 30 on return → faded and disposed) and "met 84%" counts any gaze on "stay", including the default resting gaze and after the answer has gone. Counting only the answer while it exists: 61% (10 min), 54% (5 min).
6. [P1] Reach rests on a simulated reflex (the turn toward fresh writing). Without it: 5-minute reach 50% → 35%; with the line unseen at the chair, 44%. Phone reach is unmeasured (simulated phones see the whole wall and tap what they read). Report 35–44% as the bound.
7. [P2] Restraint holds (empty windows outside memories 17 → 15% at 3 min); exclude memory spans from the longest-gap measure.
8. [P2] 9 of 40 second visits replay the same heart. Prefer an unseen heart on a return.

## Cinematic director

**Verdict.** A peak at about two minutes suits a 3–5 minute visit and fixes the real problem. But the consequence doesn't last, the answer lands on the room's darks by accident, and a 10-minute visit has its last beat at about 4 minutes.

1. [P1] The answer fades (`ttl`); on a return it fades in at the hand-off in view. `ttl: 0`; on return, write it after they've once turned from the north wall.
2. [P1, verified simulated] The answer collides with the darks: in 23 of 84 answers a held silence or phase-turn dim starts 0–5 s later. Make it the cut: if the held silence is still to come, write the answer inside it on the unfaced wall so the returning light finds it; otherwise keep the dark ≥20 s from the answer.
3. [P1] The second half has no shape: last structural beat at a median 233 s; the door drops 10% → 0%. One peak, then an epilogue: let the door (on its own wall, 18 s, "someone comes to the door and stops") come after the heart from 360 s, ≥150 s after the answer, just before people leave.
4. [P1] The bedroom is inverted: 6 of 10 bedroom hearts played before any held silence (the morning, then the dark). Keep `afterDark` for the bedroom; apart visitors get the silence at ~122–140 s anyway.
5. [P1] The woodland as heart doubles its ending (coda, then the answer 29 s later), and its `lastMusic` now takes the room's music from ~2.5 min in 19% of visits (was 4%). Almost and Things don't break as a first memory.
6. [P1] The card plays the bookend as a couplet (one `<p>`, one fade). Stage it: first line alone at +1.5 s; the answer at +6.5 s, a blank line below, 0.85em, upright, pencil grey; button and audio suspend at +10.5 s; reduced motion +1.5/+2.5/+4 s.
7. [P2] The graphite sound ~0.4 s before the second line.

## Skeptic (on the proposal and the three reviews)

**Verdict.** The heart bundles two things: easier gates for one strong memory (measured and real) and a four-way "rhyme" (mostly invisible). "A visitor can only feel a rhyme between two things they actually saw." Only one branch is like that: the room recoils when the visitor comes back (−14.5% of the frame, core-loop), then Almost shows a hand taken back. The prototype gave Almost to only 13–15% of the visitors it recoiled at, because their heart was fixed at about 60 s from another shape.

1. [P1, verified] The recoil happens in 56/77/85% of 3/5/10-minute visits; Almost followed it in 14/13/15% (`scratchpad/skeptic/simple.ts`).
2. [P1, verified] The extra reach comes from the eased gates, not the rhyme (Almost-for-everyone reached more). `loosen` was not a fair control (it still needed `free`, the 120 s spacing and the two-line cap).
3. [P1, verified] The simplest version works: Almost only after a recoil reaches 24/36/45% of visits, 42–54% of the recoiled.
4. [P1, verified] About two-thirds of the gain survives removing the simulated turn toward fresh writing (heart 42 → 26% at 3 min, 50 → 36% at 5 min).
5. [P1] The other three rhymes exist only in the inspector: lengths of time, not moments a visitor remembers. The core-loop round had decided "kept for Almost only".
6. [P1] The interaction designer's visibility fix is essential.
- Answer lines: keep only "I took my hand back.". Card: only what the wall shows, one line break, at most a CSS delay. Kitchen light: neither after the heart nor lost everywhere; its gates for visits with no recoil. "Came back" = the recoil, the only trigger.
- **KEEP:** Almost after a recoil with the heart's gates; nothing painted after it; "I took my hand back."; the answer kept for the next visit; the line placed where it can be seen; the voice check.
- **CHANGE:** the trigger to the recoil; the card only if the wall has the answer; the kept answer present without being written in view; re-inking only if >30% are still never begun after the visibility fix.
- **CUT:** the woodland, Things and bedroom hearts and their answers; the door epilogue; the kitchen light after the heart; the answer inside the held silence; the staged card and the graphite sound; preferring an unseen heart on return; the stillness requirement in the gaze gate; the `loosen` and `heartAlmost` scaffolding.

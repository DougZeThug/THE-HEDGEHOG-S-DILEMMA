# After: the finished heart, before against after (simulated)

`ARMS='before=noHeart;after' npx vite-node scripts/arc.ts`, run on the finished build (after QA's fixes, the creative director's cuts and the fixes from the pull request's code review: chiefly that the heart is offered only into its local quiet, and, let be, written again on the wall it was written on). Both arms carry the fix for a return lost to the visit's turn, which is why "recoiled at" in the before arm is a little higher than in `brief.md`. The core-loop battery (`N=12 SEEDS=3 RETURNS=20 ARMS='before=noHeart;after' npx vite-node scripts/experiment.ts`, 688 visits) is unchanged within noise between the arms. "Answer in view" is the Director's own measure (the answer's wall faced, in the simulation); the simulated visitors can't perceive a later annotation, so whether anyone looks at it is not measured. Likewise "last visit's answer back on the wall" on a second visit counts the room writing it there (`heart-kept`), not a look at it. "First strong" is the first of Almost, Things and the woodland to begin, whether or not it is the heart.

1592 visits in 60.6 s. Simulated visitors; their reactions are assumptions.

    What the measurements mean
    

      
They show whether takes differ, not whether they are convincing. Only listening can decide that.

      
range pitch range between the 10th and 90th percentile, in semitones. The older takes sit near 3–6.

      
pause silence inside the line, in seconds.

      
voiced share of speech with a clear pitch. Low values mean breath, whisper, laughter or a cracking voice.

      
dyn loudness range across the line, in dB.

      
ends pitch at the end compared with the middle, in semitones. Positive rises, negative falls.

      
heard what speech-to-text transcribed, including detected laughs or sighs. A take that adds words to the authored line is flagged.

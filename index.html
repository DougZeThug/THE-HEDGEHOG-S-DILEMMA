how to be in the room
  • W A S D or ↑ ↓ to walk, ← → or the mouse to look. Shift to go slower.
  • F walks you gently toward whatever you are looking at. C turns you to the chair.
  • Near the chair, Space or a click lets you kneel.
  • T to leave something of your own on the wall.
  • Esc for sound and captions, comfort and quality settings, and to leave.
  • On a touch screen: drag on the right to look, on the left to walk, or tap the floor to go there. Tap writing to read it closely; tap the chair to go to it, and again to kneel. ··· (bottom right) for sound and captions, comfort and quality, and to leave.
  • Without a mouse: Tab turns you toward the next piece of writing.
